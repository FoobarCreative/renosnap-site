Renosnap 1.0 is now live on the App Store, giving homeowners a way to explore renovation ideas with a snap and a tap.

Built for everyday renovation decisions, idea building and home decor styling, Renosnap helps users upload a photo of their space and generate design directions in seconds. The app is designed to make early planning more visual and less intimidating, whether someone is refreshing a single room or rethinking an entire home.

Version 1.0 introduces the core Renosnap experience:

- AI-powered interior redesign concepts from a single photo
- Exterior visualizations for facades, paint, and curb appeal ideas
- Landscape concepts for gardens, patios, and outdoor living areas
- Staging tools to help users imagine empty rooms with furniture and decor
- Magic Brush editing for targeted changes inside a scene
- Before-and-after comparisons for evaluating renovation directions side by side

The launch of Renosnap 1.0 marks the beginning of a broader product roadmap focused on simplifying renovation planning for homeowners. Future updates will continue expanding design workflows, planning tools, and practical guidance that help users move from idea to execution with more confidence.

Renosnap is available now on the App Store.

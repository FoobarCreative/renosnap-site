Bathrooms are compact but expensive: high labour density, waterproofing rules, and fixture choices add up quickly.

## Typical bathroom budget ranges (AUD)

- **Basic refresh:** $12,000–$20,000  
- **Mid-range renovation:** $20,000–$35,000  
- **Premium renovation:** $35,000–$55,000+

## What pushes bathroom costs up

- Waterproofing and compliance requirements
- Tile format/complexity (large format, patterns, niches)
- Plumbing relocations
- Custom shower screens/vanities
- Ventilation and mould remediation

## Budget traps to avoid

- Starting demolition before final selections are locked
- Underestimating waterproofing and prep work
- Choosing incompatible fixtures late in the build
- No contingency for hidden water damage

## Quick budgeting rule of thumb

For planning purposes, assume:
- **10–15% contingency** for straightforward jobs
- **15–20% contingency** for older homes or full strip-outs

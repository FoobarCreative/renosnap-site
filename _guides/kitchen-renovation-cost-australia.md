If you're planning a kitchen renovation, the fastest way to blow your budget is under-scoping the project.

## Typical kitchen budget ranges (AUD)

- **Cosmetic refresh:** $18,000–$30,000  
  (paint, handles, minor joinery updates, splashback refresh)
- **Mid-range full renovation:** $30,000–$55,000  
  (new cabinetry, benchtops, appliances, lighting, some services relocation)
- **Premium renovation:** $55,000–$90,000+  
  (layout changes, premium finishes, integrated appliances, structural works)

## Cost drivers that matter most

1. **Layout changes** (moving sink/cooktop is expensive)  
2. **Cabinetry material + hardware quality**  
3. **Stone selection and edge profiles**  
4. **Electrical upgrades and switchboard constraints**  
5. **Trade access and demolition complexity**

## Commonly missed budget items

- Site protection and rubbish removal
- Temporary kitchen setup
- Unexpected subfloor/wall repairs
- Permit/certification requirements
- 10–20% contingency

## Smart planning sequence

1. Define must-haves vs nice-to-haves
2. Set a hard cap budget with contingency included
3. Get 2–3 itemised quotes
4. Confirm inclusions/exclusions in writing
5. Use Renosnap to test style directions before committing
